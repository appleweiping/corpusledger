# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating claims
on evidence, give actionable feedback, and protect other people's private data. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository's security/contact channel. Maintainers will review reports promptly, preserve
confidentiality where possible, and take proportionate corrective action.
